## Pitch rules (from the template)

- **Format:** 6 minutes presentation + 4 minutes Q&A.
- **Structure:** 7 core slides, in this order:

  | # | Slide | Suggested time |
  |---|---|---|
  | - | Cover page | 20 sec |
  | 1 | The Idea | 20 sec |
  | 2 | The Problem | 50 sec |
  | 3 | Our Solution | 60 sec |
  | 4 | What Makes It Innovative? | 50 sec |
  | 5 | How It Works & Feasibility | 60 sec |
  | 6 | Impact & Potential | 60 sec |
  | 7 | Why Us + Closing Vision | 40 sec |

- **Judging criteria:**

  | Criterion | Weight |
  |---|---|
  | Innovation | 30% |
  | Feasibility | 25% |
  | Social / Economic Impact | 25% |
  | Pitch Quality / Execution | 20% |

## Before submitting

- Delete the instruction slides ("Before you start" and "Final submission checklist").
- Remove all instructional and placeholder text.
- The template footer says "URIIS Student Biz Innov Challenge 2026 | Final Pitch". Check with the organisers whether to keep it or change it to this event's name.
- Check spelling, names, numbers and sources.
- Rehearse so the pitch finishes within 6 minutes.
