# GUIDELINE NO. 2 OF 2025 (GARIS PANDUAN BIL. 2/2025)

Title: Cloud-First Guideline 2025
Issued by: Pejabat Ketua Pegawai Digital Contoh (Sintetik)
Reference: GP 2/2025
Date issued: 15 January 2025
Effective date: 1 February 2025
Classification: TERBUKA

## PURPOSE

1. This guideline makes cloud the first choice for new government systems while keeping the most sensitive information on-premise.

## SCOPE

2. This guideline applies to all federal ministries, departments and agencies, and to their ICT vendors.

## CLOUD USE BY CLASSIFICATION

3. Where information may be stored depends on its security classification:

3.1 Information classified Terbuka may be stored in any approved cloud service.

3.2 Information classified Terhad may be stored in approved in-country cloud regions with the approval of the Chief Digital Officer.

3.3 Information classified Sulit may be stored in approved in-country cloud regions only if it is encrypted with keys held by the Government.

3.4 Information classified Rahsia or Rahsia Besar must remain on-premise in the Government Data Centre.

## APPROVAL PROCESS

4. Agencies submit the Cloud Use Application Form to the Chief Digital Officer, who decides within 14 working days of receiving a complete application.

## SUPERSESSION

5. This guideline supersedes the Guideline on the Use of Public Cloud Services, Garis Panduan Bil. 1/2022, with effect from 1 February 2025.

## EFFECTIVE DATE

6. This guideline takes effect on 1 February 2025.
