# GUIDELINE NO. 1 OF 2022 (GARIS PANDUAN BIL. 1/2022)

Title: Guideline on the Use of Public Cloud Services 2022
Issued by: Pejabat Ketua Pegawai Digital Contoh (Sintetik)
Reference: GP 1/2022
Date issued: 10 March 2022
Effective date: 1 April 2022
Classification: TERBUKA

## PURPOSE

1. This guideline sets the rules for using public cloud services in federal government agencies.

## SCOPE

2. This guideline applies to all federal ministries, departments and agencies, and to their ICT vendors.

## RULES FOR CLOUD USE

3. The following rules apply:

3.1 Only information classified Terbuka (public) may be stored in a public cloud service.

3.2 All official information classified Terhad or above must remain on-premise in the Government Data Centre.

3.3 Agencies must obtain the approval of the Chief Digital Officer before subscribing to any cloud service. The Chief Digital Officer decides within 30 working days.

## EFFECTIVE DATE

4. This guideline takes effect on 1 April 2022.
