# Hybrid Work Policy 2025

Issued by: Human Resource Management Division, Kerajaan Negeri Contoh
Reference: DASAR-HR 1/2025
Date issued: 3 March 2025
Effective date: 1 April 2025

Note: Kerajaan Negeri Contoh is a fictional government. All names, numbers and dates in this document are invented for demonstration only.

## 1. Purpose

This policy lets State Government staff combine office work and remote work to improve productivity and well-being.

## 2. Eligibility

Confirmed staff whose duties can be done remotely are eligible. Front-counter staff may join on a rotation basis decided by their department.

## 3. Remote Work Days

3.1 Eligible staff may work remotely for up to 3 days per week.

3.2 Each team must make sure at least one officer is in the office on every working day.

## 4. Approval

4.1 Hybrid work arrangements are approved by the Head of Department.

4.2 Each approved arrangement is reviewed every 6 months.

## 5. Core Hours

Staff working remotely must be reachable by phone and official chat between 10.00 am and 3.00 pm.

## 6. Equipment and Security

6.1 Staff must use the official VPN and an approved device.

6.2 Confidential and Secret documents must not be printed at home.

## 7. Effective Date

This policy takes effect on 1 April 2025.
